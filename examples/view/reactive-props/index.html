Fitz reactive props — Phase 11.7.a smoke

An App.fitzv parent passes two of its own state fields down to a <Badge /> child as interpolated props (heading="{title}", count="{clicks + 1}"). Click bump the parent — the parent's clicks flows into the child's count prop, live. Reactive propagation falls out of the dirty-flag re-render; no signals, no VDOM.
